I'm a designer with 5 years of experience, mostly working with startups — helping them go from a rough idea to something that actually ships. I've worked on apps, dashboards, EdTech platforms, branding, the whole mix.

I care more about whether something works than whether it looks good. That means I ask a lot of questions upfront, move fast, and don't drag projects into endless revision cycles.

I'm currently expanding into design engineering — writing the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript behind the things I design. Because if you can both design and build, you can work faster, communicate better, and ship products that don't lose something in translation.

When I'm not designing, I'm out taking random photos or finding roads nobody else seems to bother with.
